Privacy Policy

Irtiqaa · Last updated March 2026

Short version: Irtiqaa does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Your journeys, habits, and progress stay entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Irtiqaa stores all app data locally on your device using SQLite. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or track usage. The following data exists only on your device:

  • Journey plans, programs, templates, and tasks you create
  • Daily habit records and progress
  • App preferences (theme, language, reminders)
  • AI provider selection

2. AI-Generated Plans

When you use the "Generate with AI" feature, the goal text you enter is sent to the AI provider you have selected in Settings:

  • Apple Intelligence — processed entirely on-device; nothing leaves your iPhone.
  • Claude (Anthropic) — your goal text is sent to Anthropic's API. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI) — your goal text is sent to OpenAI's API. See openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • Gemini (Google) — your goal text is sent to Google's API. See policies.google.com/privacy.

No other data (journeys, tasks, records) is sent to any AI provider.

3. API Keys

API keys you enter for cloud AI providers are stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are never transmitted to any server other than the corresponding AI provider's API endpoint during plan generation.

4. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Irtiqaa requests permission to send local notifications. All notification scheduling is handled on-device by iOS. No notification data is transmitted externally.

5. Third-Party Services

Irtiqaa does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only external network requests are AI API calls, which are initiated explicitly by you when generating a plan.

6. Children's Privacy

Irtiqaa is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@irtiqaa.app.
